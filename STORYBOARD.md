# Muse / Jolly — storyboard

Logline: Sam wants to take their partner to dinner but is swamped, so they hand it to Muse (Jolly), who finds a table, checks with Sam, and books it.
Takeaway: you ask → Muse handles it → it checks with you → done.

World: lavender dusk living room → inside the phone (app blue) → over-the-shoulder at night, warming on ✓ → amber candlelit restaurant.
Motif: the dream bubble (plate, candle, partner, heart) in A is exactly the dinner in D. The phone: Jolly comes out of it in A and peeks out of it in D.

## Shots

### A  0–7s  The ask  [iris in → push into the phone + blue fade]
- 0.0–0.6  iris opens on Sam, slumped on the sofa: laptop full of unread rows, a pile of papers
- 0.7–1.9  work pings pop off Sam's phone; sigh
- 2.1–3.6  Sam looks up, dream bubble: dinner for two with the partner
- 3.9      Sam taps the phone → 4.1 Jolly pops out (sparkles), waves
- 4.8–5.9  Sam points at the bubble; Jolly looks, idea bulb
- 6.1–7.0  Jolly zips back into the phone; camera dives in

### B  7–14s  Muse works (inside the phone)  [blue fade → cream fade]

- 9.25–10.3 the candlelit place: 5 stars, gold glow; it flies to the corner

- 12.6      free slot pops gold with ✓ → Jolly surprised, then excited

### C  14–21s  Checks with you  [cream fade → party brush wipe]

### D  21–30s  Done  [wipe → iris out]

- 26.3–28.5 love → happy wave
- 28.5–29.85 iris closes on Jolly
