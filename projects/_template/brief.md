# Brief: <product> launch film

Fill in every section. The agent storyboards from this, so anything missing gets invented.

- **Product:** what it is, in one sentence a stranger would understand.
- **The one message:** the single thing the viewer must remember. One sentence.
- **Audience:** who watches, and what they already know.
- **Length and format:** seconds; 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16; where it's posted; sound on or off (if off, captions carry
  everything).
- **Genre:** one of docs/genres/ (UI walkthrough, type-led, screen demo, brand and graphic, 3D, illustrated, abstract,
  footage with overlays).
- **Call to action:** the button or line, and the URL.
- **Must include:** features to show, claims or numbers (with their source), a customer line, a partner logo.
- **Must avoid:** claims you can't make, competitors, styles the brand dislikes.
- **Brand:** accent colour, font, logo file, light or dark, and any guidelines (link or attach).
- **Assets supplied:** each file in assets/ with a line on what it is (and, for recordings, the moments to use: "0:12
  clicks Share").
- **References:** 1–3 films whose feel you want, from whatships.com or elsewhere, and what about each one.
