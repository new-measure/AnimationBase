# Brief: Tandem launch film (demo project)

A stand-in brief that exercises the Phase 3 pipeline. Real projects fill in the same sections; the agent storyboards
from it (see projects/README.md).

- **Product:** Tandem, which writes your team's status updates from the work itself.
- **The one message:** your team stays in sync without writing updates.
- **Audience:** team leads at software companies.
- **Length and format:** about 20 s, 16:9, for X and LinkedIn; sound on, captions burned in.
- **Genre:** footage-led with motion graphics over it (docs/genres/footage_overlays.md), with a screen-recording demo
  inside (docs/genres/screen_demo.md).
- **Call to action:** "Start free", tandem.example.
- **Must include:** the product's real screen recording; a line from a customer (captioned); the logo end card.
- **Brand:** accent #0E7C66, logo assets/logo.svg, Inter.
- **Assets supplied:** assets/broll.mp4 (footage), assets/screen.mp4 (screen recording, 8 s), assets/music.wav (bed),
  assets/logo.svg.
